| 2026-04-20 | Deletion-content preservation guard on `upsert_post` / `upsert_comment` — **content-heuristic, not flag-based** | Live probes P1 / P2 (`verification_probes.md`) confirmed the worst case: Moltbook returns tombstoned posts and comments as `content='[deleted]'` with `is_deleted:false`.
